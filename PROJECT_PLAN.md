# PROJECT_PLAN.md — BHARAT: Build the Civilization

## 1. Core Philosophy & Player Fantasy
You are the Chief Architect and Sovereign of a nascent settlement along the sacred river basin of ancient Bharat. You establish fertile farmsteads, quarry sandstone, fire terracotta bricks, erect stupendous citadels, research ancient sciences, train warrior guards, send expeditions across subcontinental horizons, and uncover archaeological discoveries.

## 2. Five Centralized Resources
| Resource | Primary Production | Primary Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FOOD (Dhanya)** | Farmlands / Granary | Population sustenance, worker labor, army troop training |
| **WOOD (Kashta)** | Timber Yard / Sal Grove | Building frameworks, palisades, scaffolding, carts |
| **STONE (Shila)** | Stone Yard / Rocky Outcrop | Defensive walls, citadel platforms, stepped structures |
| **CLAY (Mrittika)**| Clay Workshop / Riverbank | Fired bricks (1:2:4 ratio), hydraulic linings, granary urns |
| **KNOWLEDGE (Vidya)** | Knowledge Center / Scribe Huts | Unlocking technologies, discovering regional sites |

## 3. The 10 Core Buildings
1. **Civilization Center (Raja Sabha)**: Central citadel. Determines max civilization level, unlocks new building types and building slots.
2. **Granary (Koshagara)**: Protects grain from weather and pests; expands Food storage capacity.
3. **Timber Yard (Darushala)**: Collects and saws seasoned hardwood; produces Wood continuously.
4. **Stone Yard (Shilpashala)**: Cuts granite and sandstone blocks; produces Stone continuously.
5. **Clay Workshop (Kumbhashala)**: Extracts river clay and fires terracotta bricks; produces Clay continuously.
6. **Knowledge Center (Gurukula)**: Scribes and scholars study astronomy, mathematics, and metallurgy; generates Knowledge.
7. **Training Ground (Akhada)**: Martial pavilion for training defensive infantry, archers, and scout cavalry.
8. **Market Square (Vipani)**: Exchange excess materials and engage with travelling merchant caravans.
9. **Water Structure (Jalashaya / Baoli)**: Stepped reservoir / canal; boosts agricultural fertility and population capacity.
10. **Craft House (Karmashala)**: Metal smithing, carnelian bead cutting, and tool fabrication.

## 4. The 8-Step Onboarding Architecture
- **Step 1: The Founding**: Welcome to Bharat. Name your settlement and inspect the fertile river basin.
- **Step 2: First Harvest**: Tap the sacred Sal tree grove to gather timber (Wood).
- **Step 3: Laying Bricks**: Build a Clay Workshop on an open plot.
- **Step 4: Sovereign Expansion**: Upgrade the Civilization Center to Level 2.
- **Step 5: Ancient Wisdom**: Enter the Knowledge Center and research Brickmaking (Pakva-Ishtika).
- **Step 6: Guardians of the Basin**: Construct the Training Ground and recruit your first Padati (Spearmen).
- **Step 7: Royal Decree**: Complete the first civic mission and claim royal knowledge rewards.
- **Step 8: Beyond the River**: Open the Regional Expedition Map to survey the Indus-Saraswati horizons.
