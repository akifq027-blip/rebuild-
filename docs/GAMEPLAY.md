# BHARAT — BUILD THE CIVILIZATION
## Gameplay Systems & Mechanics

### 1. The Core Loop
```
EXPLORE  →  COLLECT  →  LEARN  →  BUILD  →  DISCOVER  →  PROGRESS
```

### 2. Resources & Storage
- **Food**: Sustains community population growth and expedition foraging.
- **Water**: Essential for settlement health, bricks, and irrigation.
- **Wood**: Sourced from sacred groves; frames houses and workshops.
- **Stone**: Quarrried from river gravel; foundations, hearths, and wells.
- **Metal**: Copper/bronze/iron nuggets for tools and architectural nails.
- **Knowledge**: Gained via exploration, challenges, and missions to unlock technologies.
- **Culture**: Earned via dilemmas and traditions to fuel civil identity.
- **Trade**: Sourced from surplus harvests and maritime exchange.

### 3. Settlement Construction
- Eight settlement slots arranged around a communal hearth.
- Buildings: Huts (population capacity +2), Farms (+5 passive food), Wells (+5 passive water), Storage (+250 max storage), Workshops (+2 passive knowledge).

### 4. Technology Tree
- Branching historical progression:
  - **Fire Mastery** → **Agriculture** → **Pottery** → **Tools** → **Water Hydrology** → **Metallurgy** → **Astronomy & Math**.
- Each technology presents both **Gameplay Effects** and **Historical Context**.

### 5. Missions & Achievements
- Pedagogical milestones rewarding XP, knowledge, and culture.
- Achievements track mastery across building, exploration, technology, artifacts, and eras.

### 6. Journey Through Time
- 11 historical eras spanning from Early Neolithic settlements to the British Colonial Period and Swadeshi self-reliance.
