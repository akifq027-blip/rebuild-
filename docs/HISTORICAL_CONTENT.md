# BHARAT — BUILD THE CIVILIZATION
## Historical Content, Chapters & Academic Sources

### 1. The 11 Broad Historical Chapters
1. **Early Settlements** (c. 7000–2600 BCE): Mehrgarh mud-brick architecture, barley/wheat cultivation, cattle pastoralism.
2. **Indus / Harappan Civilization** (c. 2600–1900 BCE): Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Lothal; grid urbanism, covered drainage, standardized weights.
3. **Vedic Period** (c. 1500–600 BCE): Pastoral transition to agriculture, iron metallurgy (*Krishna-Ayas*), assemblies (*Sabha* and *Samiti*).
4. **Mahajanapadas** (c. 600–320 BCE): 16 urban kingdoms/republics, punch-marked coins, Northern Black Polished Ware (NBPW), philosophical inquiry.
5. **Mauryan Period** (c. 320–185 BCE): Pan-subcontinental governance, Ashokan rock edicts, stone pillars, Chanakya's *Arthashastra*.
6. **Gupta Period** (c. 320–550 CE): Classical mathematics (place-value, zero), astronomy (Aryabhata), metallurgy (Delhi Iron Pillar).
7. **Medieval India** (c. 600–1200 CE): Monumental temple architecture (Chola bronzes, Pallava monolithic rathas), tank irrigation networks.
8. **Mughal Period** (c. 1526–1707 CE): Indo-Islamic synthesis, Charbagh water gardens, Agra and Delhi red sandstone monuments, textile workshops.
9. **Regional Kingdoms and Cultural Centres** (c. 1300–1800 CE): Vijayanagara hydraulic aqueducts, Maratha hill forts, Ahom bund dykes.
10. **European Arrival & Maritime Trade** (c. 1500–1757 CE): Coromandel textile trade, spice factories, Indian Ocean trade networks.
11. **British Colonial Period & Freedom Era** (c. 1757–1947 CE): De-industrialization of handlooms, railway infrastructure, Swadeshi economic self-reliance.

### 2. Academic & Institutional Sources
- **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)**: Excavation memoirs (Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal, Kalibangan).
- **NCERT**: History Textbooks (Themes in Indian History, Parts 1, 2, and 3).
- **National Museum, New Delhi**: Archaeological galleries and artifact accession catalogs.
- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre**: Inscriptions for Dholavira, Rani ki Vav, Chola Temples.
- **Epigraphia Indica**: Epigraphical records and Ashokan edict translations.

### 3. Historical Accuracy Standard
- **No False Claims**: The game is described as *"historically informed and source-backed educational gameplay."*
- **Explicit Uncertainty**: Debated topics (e.g. undeciphered Indus script, causes of Harappan de-urbanization) are clearly tagged as *Evidence is limited or interpretations differ*.
